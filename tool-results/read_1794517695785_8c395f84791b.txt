   608→### Product variants
   609→
   610→Unlike standard clothing stores, a saree store should not force every saree into size variations.
   611→
   612→Use variants based on:
   613→
   614→- Color.
   615→- Design.
   616→- Border type.
   617→- Blouse color.
   618→- Product-specific SKU.
   619→
   620→For individual one-of-a-kind sarees, use individual stock quantities, often one unit per SKU. For repeatable designs, allow multiple units.
